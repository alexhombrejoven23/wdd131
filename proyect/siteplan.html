Website Plan: FastTrack English AI Hub

WDD131 - Week 5 Project | Student: Alex Condori | Date:

Site Name & Branding

FEAI

FastTrack English AI Hub

Domain Name: fasttrackenglish.ai (available)

This name represents an intensive 6-month English learning program that leverages artificial intelligence for personalized instruction. The "FastTrack" indicates accelerated learning, "English" specifies the subject, and "AI Hub" communicates the technological innovation at the core of the program.

Site Purpose

The FastTrack English AI Hub website serves as the central information and interaction platform for my "English in 6 Months" intensive course. The site provides:

  • Clear value proposition and course overview
  • Detailed 6-month curriculum breakdown
  • Interactive AI tool demonstrations (conversation simulator, writing assistant)
  • Instructor credentials and teaching philosophy
  • Student testimonials and success stories
  • Functional enrollment and contact system

The goal is to convert visitors into enrolled students by demonstrating the effectiveness of the AI-enhanced learning approach.

Target Audience Scenarios

These scenarios represent questions from potential students that will guide site content:

  1. Working Professional: "I need to improve my business English quickly for a promotion. How does your AI-powered approach help with specific workplace communication skills?"
  2. University Student: "I'm preparing for study abroad next semester. Can I try a sample AI conversation session to see if this matches my learning style before enrolling?"

Typography

Two complementary Google Fonts selected for optimal readability:

Poppins Headings

Used for all headings, navigation, and buttons. This geometric sans-serif font conveys modernity and clarity.

Example heading: Master English in 6 Months

Open Sans Body Text

Used for all paragraphs and body content. This highly legible sans-serif font ensures comfortable reading.

Example body text: Our AI-powered approach personalizes your learning journey, adapting to your pace and focusing on your specific challenges.
