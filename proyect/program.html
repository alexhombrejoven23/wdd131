The 6-Month Program

A comprehensive, structured curriculum designed to take you from beginner to fluent English speaker

Track Your Progress

Month 1: Foundations & Survival English

Beginner

Learning Objectives

  • Master the English alphabet and pronunciation basics
  • Learn essential greetings and introductions
  • Build a foundation vocabulary of 500+ common words
  • Understand basic sentence structures (subject-verb-object)
  • Use present simple tense confidently

Key Topics

  • Personal information and introductions
  • Numbers, dates, and time
  • Family and relationships
  • Daily routines and activities
  • Shopping and basic transactions
  • Asking for directions

AI Tool Focus

The conversation simulator will practice basic greetings, introductions, and simple questions. The vocabulary quiz will focus on high-frequency everyday words.

Month 2: Building Confidence

Elementary

Learning Objectives

  • Expand vocabulary to 1,000+ words
  • Master past simple and future simple tenses
  • Form and answer questions naturally
  • Tell simple stories about past events
  • Express likes, dislikes, and preferences

Key Topics

  • Food, restaurants, and ordering
  • Hobbies and free time activities
  • Weather and seasons
  • Transportation and travel
  • Health and common ailments
  • Making plans and appointments

AI Tool Focus

Practice ordering food, making appointments, and narrating past experiences with the AI simulator. Writing assistant helps with simple email composition.

Month 3: Intermediate Communication

Pre-Intermediate

Learning Objectives

  • Use present perfect and continuous tenses
  • Understand and use common phrasal verbs
  • Express opinions and give reasons
  • Understand main points of native speaker conversations
  • Write coherent paragraphs

Key Topics

  • Work and career discussions
  • Technology and social media
  • Education and learning
  • Entertainment and culture
  • Environment and social issues (basic)
  • Making suggestions and giving advice

AI Tool Focus

Engage in opinion-based conversations. Writing assistant provides feedback on paragraph structure and coherence.

Month 4: Advanced Expression

Intermediate

Learning Objectives

  • Master all verb tenses and aspects
  • Use conditional sentences (first, second, third)
  • Understand and use idioms and colloquialisms
  • Recognize different accents and speaking styles
  • Write formal and informal texts appropriately

Key Topics

  • News and current events
  • Personal experiences and storytelling
  • Hypothetical situations
  • Cultural differences and customs
  • Abstract concepts and ideas
  • Debating and persuasion

AI Tool Focus

Practice debates, hypothetical scenarios, and storytelling. Writing assistant helps refine style and tone for different audiences.

Month 5: Professional Skills

Upper-Intermediate

Learning Objectives

  • Master business English vocabulary
  • Write professional emails and reports
  • Deliver clear presentations
  • Participate confidently in meetings
  • Negotiate and persuade professionally

Key Topics

  • Business correspondence
  • Meetings and conference calls
  • Presentations and public speaking
  • Networking and small talk
  • Project management communication
  • Customer service interactions

AI Tool Focus

Simulate job interviews, presentations, and business meetings. Writing assistant provides advanced feedback on professional documents.

Month 6: Fluency & Mastery

Advanced

Learning Objectives

  • Achieve natural, fluent conversation
  • Use advanced vocabulary and expressions
  • Understand nuanced meanings and implications
  • Write with sophisticated style and precision
  • Adapt language to any social or professional context

Key Topics

  • Specialized vocabulary for your field
  • Advanced writing techniques
  • Humor and sarcasm in English
  • Complex negotiations and diplomacy
  • Academic and research language
  • Leadership communication

AI Tool Focus

Practice complex scenarios in your specific field. Writing assistant helps polish advanced documents to native-speaker quality.
